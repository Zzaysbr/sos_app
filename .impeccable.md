## Design Context

### Users
Emergency users asking for help, volunteers and people carrying messages offline. The creator explicitly requests that ordinary app users can easily see the source device, intermediaries/hops and delivery status. They may be hurried or stressed.

### Brand Personality
Clear, calm, dependable. Preserve Emergency's existing theme. Do not imply official rescue dispatch, verified human identity, guaranteed delivery or read receipts.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the existing native Flutter visual language, prominent device/status icons and a simple ordered route, supported by short readable text. The creator chose editable nicknames plus a short device code, and icons more prominent than text. Nicknames are local labels; never infer manufacturer/model from an installation key. Thai and English, narrow phones and large text must work.

### Design Principles
- Put source, known intermediate count and delivery stage before technical details.
- Distinguish stored on a relay, received on this device, and destination storage confirmation.
- Label partial routes and unknown counts explicitly; never draw an invented complete path.
- Use icons plus text, and a vertical route that can wrap and scroll.
- Keep existing history/media and protocol semantics intact.
